BWRX-300 interactive presentation (the ~7-minute talk, plus the 48 s film)
==========================================================================

Lithuanian instructions (the main ones): START-HERE.txt / START-HERE-LT.txt

This folder runs from a USB drive or any local folder: no internet, no installation, no server.

  BWRX300.html               the presentation (everything is inside this one file)
  BWRX300-fallback-lt.mp4    the 48 s film as a video, in Lithuanian (1080p, 30 fps, no sound)
  BWRX300-fallback-en.mp4    the same video in English
  START-HERE.txt             instructions in Lithuanian (START-HERE-LT.txt is the same file)
  START-HERE-EN.txt          this file


Before the talk
---------------
1. Copy the whole BWRX300 folder to the computer (or run it from the USB drive).
2. Open BWRX300.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge:
   right-click > Open with > Chrome / Edge, or drag the file onto an open browser window.
3. Press F11 for full screen (or move the mouse and click "Full screen", bottom right).
4. Wait until the loading screen has gone. The first frame of the plant is the "ready" picture.
   The page opens in Low quality: the setting the talk was tested with.

Language: the presentation starts in Lithuanian. For English, move the mouse and click EN in the
controls before starting (LT switches back). The choice is remembered on this computer.


During the talk
---------------
  Right arrow / Space / Page Down   next step (the first press starts the talk)
  Left arrow / Page Up              previous step
  Home                              back to the opening picture
  R                                 re-establish the current step (use this after any accidental key)
  Esc                               leave the talk for free exploration; Right arrow returns to the same step
  F11                               full screen on / off

Presenter clickers work: most send Page Down / Page Up.
Every step waits for the next key; nothing moves on by itself, and a second press while the picture
is still moving is ignored. The closing picture stays on screen for the questions.
The controls only appear when the mouse moves, and never during the talk.
The picture keeps its 16:9 shape on any projector; 4:3 or 16:10 screens show black bars.

The 48 s film is still there: move the mouse and click "48 s film" in the controls. While it plays,
Space pauses / resumes, Home restarts it, and R stops it and goes back to the start view.


If the motion is not smooth
---------------------------
The page already opens in Low quality. On a very slow computer, lower the render resolution: in the
address bar, add
    ?quality=low&scale=0.8
to the end of the address (after BWRX300.html) and press Enter. (scale accepts 0.5 to 1.)


If the 3D view does not start
-----------------------------
The page shows a short message with "Play the backup video" (in the chosen language). You can also
open BWRX300-fallback-en.mp4 directly with any video player (Windows Media Player, VLC, the browser).
The backup video is the 48 s film, not the whole talk.


Browsers tested (Windows 11, Intel Iris Plus graphics, opened directly from the folder)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The talk:    Chrome 155   Low quality: 60 frames per second on every step     recommended
  The 48 s film (earlier test):
               Chrome 155   works; Low quality plays smoothly (no slow frames)    recommended
               Edge 155     works; Low quality plays smoothly (no slow frames)    recommended
               Firefox 158  works, but plays less smoothly (35 to 46 fps in Low on the test machine)
